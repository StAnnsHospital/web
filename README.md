# St. Ann’s Multi-Specialty Hospital

A modern, patient-first website for St. Ann’s Cancer and General Hospital. The site helps patients and caregivers:

- **Find care:** browse departments and doctors, view profiles, and book appointments (online/phone/WhatsApp).
- **Plan visits:** check OPD and visiting hours, get directions/parking, and access admission/discharge guides.
- **Access services:** view diagnostics and health packages, download reports, and reach emergency/ambulance 24×7.
- **Understand costs:** see tariffs, insurance empanelments, and government schemes (e.g., cashless coverage).
- **Stay informed:** read news, events, and health education articles, plus community/CSR initiatives.
- **Trust & safety:** clear display of accreditations and mandatory notices; privacy-first and accessible by design.

**Design principles:** fast on low-bandwidth devices, WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility, multilingual support, SEO-ready metadata/structured data, and integration points for HIS/LIS/RIS, payments, and analytics—while respecting patient privacy and regional compliance requirements.
